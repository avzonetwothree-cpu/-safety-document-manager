SAFETY DOCUMENT MANAGER — ANDROID PWA

Files: index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png.

INSTALL:
1. Host the complete folder on an HTTPS website or approved internal HTTPS server.
2. Open index.html on your Android phone in Chrome.
3. Tap ⋮ > Install app or Add to Home screen.
4. Open from the home-screen icon. The app shell is cached for offline use after the first successful visit.

FEATURES:
- Document register: categories, status, project, contractor, reference, revision, issue/review/expiry dates, owner, notes/link, optional attachment (2 MB max).
- Expiry/review dashboard and search/filter.
- Action/NCI/hazard/inspection/audit/investigation tracker with owner, due date, status and close-out.
- Ten-section monthly report checklist based on the supplied Safety Officer Monthly Report Rev001 2026.
- CSV exports and full JSON backup/restore.

LIMITATIONS & DATA:
Local-first prototype. Records are stored in this browser on this device. No cloud sync, login, encryption layer, or company document-system integration. Export backups regularly. Do not store confidential or classified material unless your organisation approves. This app is not an official Valterra system and does not replace the controlled document system or official monthly report.
